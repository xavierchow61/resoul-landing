# Resoul — Stitch Screen Prompts

配合 `DESIGN.md` 使用。逐個貼入 Stitch 的「generate screen from text」，deviceType 選 **MOBILE**，並套用同一個 design system 以保持一致。

---

## 1. Landing — Hero
A calm, warm memorial landing hero for a pet-loss emotional-support brand "Resoul". Off-white cream background, generous whitespace. Small logo (a line-drawn paw with a heart) + italic tagline "Your last greatest love to show". A gentle rhetorical serif headline "當家裡忽然安靜下來，你，還好嗎？", a soft supporting sentence, one pill CTA button in dark sepia labeled「查看Resoul官方資料」with a right arrow, and a large 16:9 photo of an owner quietly holding a pet photo and collar. Muted, tender, no sales tone.

## 2. Emotional Support（三步）
A three-step emotional-support section on cream background. Section title「失去毛孩的那一刻，主人需要的不只是流程。」Three soft cards with serif numerals 01 / 02 / 03: 先承接失落 / 再整理下一步 / 最後留下紀念, each with a short empathetic sentence. Rounded 14px cards, thin borders, faint gold paw-print watermark.

## 3. 陪自己走過（子標題）+ 點一盞燭光
A tender interactive section titled「為毛孩，點一盞燭光。」with a subheading intro. A small form (name field, one-line message textarea, a pill button「🕯️ 點一盞燭光」) and below it a grid of small candle cards, each showing a soft animated flame, a pet name and a short message. Warm cream, gentle.

## 4. 毛孩回憶卡
A "memory card" generator section「為牠，做一張回憶卡。」An editor column (upload-photo button, name, dates, a few words of memory) on the left and a live preview card on the right/below: square photo, pet name in serif, dates, memory text, and italic brand line "Resoul · 願想念被溫柔安放". A soft "儲存這張回憶卡" button.

## 5. 紀念產品
A memorial-products lifestyle section on cream. A 16:9 media block (video/photo) and three product-context cards with photos: 把想念留在身邊 / 讓紀念被細心製作 / 為家中留一個位置. Focus on remembrance and everyday presence, not selling.

## 6. 常見心情（Accordion）
An accordion FAQ titled「常見心情」grouped by theme with small icon subheadings (剛面對失去 / 自責麻木與難過 / 想念與紀念 / 慢慢向前). Thin line dividers, a leading ＋ that turns to × on open, the open item becomes a soft rounded card with a gentle answer. Emotional-support tone.

## 7. 支援資源
A referral-resources page「當你需要，這裡有人可以幫你。」Grouped resource cards: 專業情緒支援 / 危機24小時熱線 / 獸醫支援 / 紀念工作室 / 社群支持. Each item has a bold name and a phone number styled as a tappable link. A gentle footnote about seeking help in crisis.

## 8. 收尾 + Footer
A closing section with a dark warm-sepia band: serif headline「你不需要一個人承受這段告別。」a soft supporting line, and one outlined light CTA「查看Resoul官方資料」. Below, a dark footer with the Resoul logo (light) and the italic tagline.

## 9. 情緒傾聽（浮動聊天）
A floating emotional-support chat widget anchored bottom-left. A paw launcher pill「情緒傾聽」; when open, a soft chat panel with an avatar, warm opening message, a privacy note, quick-reply chips, chat bubbles, a typing indicator, and a text input with a round send button. Gentle, non-clinical, Traditional Chinese.
