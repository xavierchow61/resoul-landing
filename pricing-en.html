Memorial products

Priced by material and meaning.

Memorial product prices are shown individually in the Memorial store by material, size, personalisation and lead time. Items already included in a farewell journey are marked "Included" to avoid double purchase.

Payment & cancellation

Made clear before you confirm.

  • An itemised quote is provided before confirmation.
  • Payment and receipt names for the vet and RESOUL services are listed separately.
  • Pick-up, remote area, night, overweight, extra ceremony time and delivery surcharges are stated.
  • Custom and engraved products enter production only after proof approval, with the number of revisions stated.
  • How sudden medical changes, vet cancellation or a vet's advice against proceeding are handled is set out in the terms.
