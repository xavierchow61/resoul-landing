About us 中

Pet euthanasia

When love becomes
the hardest decision.

Watching a companion of many years grow frail and suffer, "letting go" may be the most loving — and most heartbreaking — thing you can do for them. This decision is never easy. You don't have to face it alone; we will walk through it gently with you.

Resoul's pet euthanasia service is performed by a registered veterinarian in a painless, peaceful way, so your companion can drift off calmly in your arms and familiar scents. Whether at home or at a clinic, we safeguard these final moments for you both, and can connect directly to cremation and memorial arrangements.

Our process

Be with them, and say goodbye well.

Every step is unhurried and never cold, giving you and your companion enough time to say goodbye.→

  1. 1

    Booking & consultation

    Call or WhatsApp; we'll carefully understand your pet's condition and your needs.

  2. 2

    Vet visit at home / clinic

    Choose home or clinic, so they stay in a familiar, reassuring place.

  3. 3

    Professional assessment & guidance

    The vet assesses carefully and gently explains each step.

  4. 4

    Time to say goodbye

    Undisturbed, unhurried time together with your companion.

  5. 5

    A peaceful passing

    Performed painlessly by a registered vet, so they sleep peacefully in your arms.

  6. 6

    Cremation & memorial

    Connect directly to Resoul's cremation service and keepsakes.
